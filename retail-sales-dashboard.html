PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding sales performance through data

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales data and identify the regions, categories, customer segments, states and time periods driving overall business performance.

The analysis was performed on a dataset containing 9,801 sales records, which were explored using Excel, Pivot Tables and dashboard visualizations.

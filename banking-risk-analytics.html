PROJECT OVERVIEW

Turning banking data into actionable risk and performance insights.

This project is being developed as a Power BI business intelligence solution that transforms banking data into an interactive analytical report. The final report will combine data preparation, modelling, DAX-driven KPIs and visual analysis to help identify meaningful customer and risk patterns.

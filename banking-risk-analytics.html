PROJECT OVERVIEW

Turning banking data into actionable risk insights.

This project transforms applicant-level banking data into a four-page Power BI decision-support dashboard. The analysis combines Power Query transformation, structured data modelling and DAX-driven KPIs to identify payment-difficulty patterns, applicant segments and application behaviour.

ProjectBanking & Risk Analytics
Primary toolMicrosoft Power BI
Dataset307,511 applicants
StatusCompleted

What does the analysis help answer?

01

02

03

Risk

Which applicant and risk segments show higher observed payment-difficulty patterns and deserve closer monitoring?

KEY FINDINGS

Observed patterns from the completed analysis.

Approximately 8.1% of the 307,511 applicants are classified with payment difficulty. Cash loans show a higher observed payment-difficulty rate of 8.3% compared with 5.5% for revolving loans. The dashboard also identifies differences across age, income and risk-indicator segments.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

From analysis to action.

Use observed payment-difficulty and risk-indicator patterns to prioritise monitoring, incorporate loan-type differences into portfolio reporting, and combine applicant segmentation with broader underwriting information for more targeted assessment.

01

Monitor higher-risk segments

Prioritise deeper review of segments with consistently elevated observed payment-difficulty rates.

02

Segment portfolio monitoring

Track loan-type and applicant-segment differences instead of relying only on an overall portfolio rate.

03

Combine risk indicators

Use age, income, credit-to-income and external-risk indicators alongside broader underwriting information.

Note: Findings are descriptive and based on observed patterns in the dataset. They indicate associations, not causation, and should be combined with broader credit-risk assessment before business decisions are made.

ANALYTICAL NOTE

Descriptive, not predictive.
